The fitting room

Know your measurements.

A useful starting point for choosing your size, understanding garment proportions, and preparing for a personalised fitting.

Home / Size Guide
Before you begin

Measure with confidence.

Use a flexible measuring tape, stand naturally, and keep the tape comfortably close to the body without pulling it tight.

01. Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level beneath your arms.

02. Waist

Measure around your natural waist without holding your breath or tightening your stomach.

03. Shoulders

Measure across the back from one shoulder point to the other, following your natural shoulder line.

04. Inseam

Measure from the top of the inner thigh to your preferred trouser hem position.

05. Sleeve

With the arm slightly bent, measure from the shoulder point to the desired cuff position.

06. Neck

Measure around the base of the neck, leaving enough room for a comfortable shirt collar.

Important: Body measurements are not the same as finished garment measurements. Fit also depends on ease, cut, fabric, and your preferred silhouette. When possible, arrange a professional fitting.
